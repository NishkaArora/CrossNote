# Roadmap

Open problems we'd like help with. Open an issue or email nishkaa@stanford.edu.

- Posts in languages other than English.
- Claims made in images and video.
- Other sources of notes and fact-checks.
- Scaling past Jetstream's 10,000-account limit, and cheaper matching.
- Measuring live precision, and how people respond to labels and replies.
- Appeals and self-service opt-out.
- Hardening against prompt injection.
